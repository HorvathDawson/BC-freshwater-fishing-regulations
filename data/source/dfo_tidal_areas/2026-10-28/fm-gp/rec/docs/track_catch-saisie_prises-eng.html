iREC Track your fishing form

iREC Track your fishing form (PDF, 213 kB)

The plain text form on this page is for your information only.

Depending on your browser settings, you may need to right click on the document link and select a ‘save’ option.

Support sustainable management of the Pacific recreational fishery!

Use this form to keep track of your fishing information during the period you were selected to report on to the internet Recreational Effort and Catch (iREC) reporting program, so it is easier to complete your report accurately. See the “iREC Reporting Requirement” box printed on your licence for your access ID and reporting period. Using this form is not required and this form does not take the place of catch records of Chinook, Halibut and Lingcod you are required to write on your licence. Submit your iREC report online using your access id before the reporting deadline which is printed on your licence.

My Reporting Period _______________ My Reporting Deadline _______________ My Access ID _______________

The reporting program will ask for catches of all species of finfish and shellfish listed online. To ensure this record provides the information needed to complete your report, note the following and see the examples on page 2:

  • Record all combinations of date, management area and fishing method, including those with no kept or released catch
  • Record the number of children with Juvenile fishing licences who fished with you and for whom you were responsible. Record their catch combined with yours.

Footnotes

Footnote 1

Management Area: If you fished in Areas 2, 19, 20, 23 or 29, please provide the subarea number as well. See area maps online.

Footnote 2

Fishing method: AB=Angling from boat, AS=angling from shore, TB= Shellfish trapping from boat, TS= Shellfish trapping from shore, BD=beach digging or hand picking, DO=Divebased or other

Lodge: Y=Yes if your fishing day orIginated from a sport fishing lodge, N=No if it did not

Footnote 4

Guide: Y=Yes if you fished with a paid guide on this fishing day, N=No if you did not

Footnote 5

Crab/Prawn trap: Y= Yes if you checked crab and/or prawn/shrimp traps on this fishing day, N=No if you did not

Footnote 6

Mark-type: For Chinook and Coho Salmon only, record Wild (unmarked), Hatchery (marked), and Unknown mark status separately, using "W", "H" and "U", respectively, to indicate which mark-type each row refers to.

Return to footnote 6 referrer

Footnote 7

Chinook size range: Record all Chinook catch above the minimum size range as LS and all released Chinook catch below the minimum size limit as SL.

Return to footnote 7 referrer

Footnote 8

Dungeness legal?: Dungeness Crab only, keep separate records of catches that were legal to harvest, i.e. of legal size or sex to harvest, using "Y" and "N" (Yes/No) to indicate which category each row refers to.
