Recreational fishing limits, openings and closures in British Columbia by fishery management area

The British Columbia recreational fishing regulations map helps you find Canadian federal recreational fishing regulations in the Pacific Ocean and other tidal waters in British Columbia as well as recreational salmon fishing regulations in B.C. For information on other species that you can fish for in British Columbia please see the B.C. Freshwater Fishing Regulations Synopsis.

Select an area on the map or in the table below to find recreational fishing limits, openings and closures and other regulations.

This map has been made possible by Canada's Pacific Salmon Strategy Initiative. It's meant for information only and should not be used for navigation or other purposes. This map does not include all fishing restrictions in effect.
