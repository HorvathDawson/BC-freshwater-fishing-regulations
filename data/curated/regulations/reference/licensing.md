## Fishing Licensing in WILD

Freshwater fishing licensing will be issued through the **Wildlife Information and Licensing Data
system (WILD)** as of the **2026-27 licence year, which starts April 1, 2026**.

**Fish and Wildlife ID (FWID):** a permanent number linked to your electronic profile in WILD. You
need an FWID to access WILD and buy fishing licences. There is no cost. You must only have one
FWID.

## Proof of Identity and Authorization

When fishing, you must produce your angling licence **and photo ID**, on request of an officer.

For **basic angling licences, classified waters licences and white sturgeon conservation
licences**, you may either state your FWID, or produce your licence (paper or electronic).

For **conservation surcharge stamps**, paper or electronic licences are acceptable — however a
**paper licence is required when retaining**:

* hatchery steelhead
* chinook
* Shuswap Lake char or rainbow trout
* Kootenay Lake rainbow trout

### If you are 16 years of age or older

* You must have a valid basic licence to sport fish for **any species of fish** in non-tidal waters
  (including salmon).
* You must purchase appropriate supplementary licences and stamps.
* You must **carry your paper licence** while sport fishing when a conservation surcharge stamp
  retention record is required.
* **Basic and supplementary licences and stamps are not valid in National Parks.**

### If you are under 16 and a resident of B.C.

* You may sport fish **without any licence or stamp** (but must abide by the regulations).
* You do not need to be accompanied by a licence holder.
* **You are entitled to your own quota of fish.**

### If you are under 16 and NOT a resident of B.C.

* You do not require any licence or stamp, but **you must be accompanied by a person 16 years or
  older who holds the appropriate licences and stamps**.
* **Any fish you keep must be counted as part of the catch and possession of your accompanying
  licence holder.**
* If you want your own quota, you must buy a licence and any necessary supplementary licences.

### If you are a resident of B.C. and disabled

You may qualify for a basic freshwater angling licence at the reduced fee of $1.14.

## Indigenous Fishing in Non-tidal Waters

> First Nations have established aboriginal rights to harvest fish from non-tidal waters for food,
> social and ceremonial purposes in their traditional areas, and treaty First Nations have treaty
> rights in relation to harvesting fish in non-tidal waters. Such uses of fish from non-tidal
> waters must be sustainable, and harvesting methods must not jeopardize safety or the use and
> enjoyment of property. Any fishing from non-tidal waters for sale or barter, in whole or in
> part, is not legal, except as authorized by regulation or where there is a demonstrated
> aboriginal or treaty right to do so.

Under the Wildlife Act "Indian" means a person who is defined as an Indian under the Indian Act
(Canada).

**If you are an Indian and a resident of B.C., you are not required to obtain any type of fishing
licence or stamp to sport fish in non-tidal waters.** Indians who are residents of B.C. and wish
to fish in non-tidal waters **outside their traditionally used areas** must do so in accordance
with the Freshwater Fishing Regulations.

**Métis:** all Métis individuals intending to sport fish in non-tidal waters **are required**,
under the Wildlife Act, to hold appropriate angling licences and stamps.

## Lost licences

You are only permitted **one annual angling licence per licence year**. If you reprint your
licence, your current licence year's retention record must be transferred to the new copy.

## Conservation Surcharges

> Your basic angling licence can be validated with up to **five annual Conservation Surcharge
> Stamps**, plus a **White Sturgeon Conservation Licence**. The stamps are valid for the licence
> year (April 1 to March 31).

### 1. Steelhead

Your basic licence must be validated with a Conservation Surcharge Stamp if you fish for steelhead
anywhere in B.C. **(whether you keep or release your catch)**. In addition, a Steelhead Stamp is
mandatory when fishing on most **Classified Waters** during certain specified periods, **even when
fishing for species other than steelhead**. Refer to the regional tables for dates and exceptions.

You must immediately record your retention of hatchery steelhead on your basic angling licence.

> **NOTE: The annual province-wide quota for hatchery steelhead is 10. All wild steelhead must be
> released.**

### 2. Non-Tidal Salmon

Required to keep a salmon of any legal size or species **(other than kokanee)** from non-tidal
waters. **A stamp is not required if you release all salmon caught.** You must immediately record
your retention of adult chinook salmon.

### 3. Kootenay Lake Rainbow Trout

Required to keep rainbow trout **over 50 cm** from the **main body of Kootenay Lake**. No stamp is
required if you release all Kootenay Lake rainbow trout over 50 cm.

### 4. Shuswap Lake Char

Required to keep char **over 60 cm** (Lake trout and Bull trout) caught in **Shuswap Lake**. No
stamp is required if you release all such char.

### 5. Shuswap Lake Rainbow Trout

Required to keep rainbow trout **over 50 cm** from **Shuswap Lake, Little Shuswap Lake, South
Thompson River between Shuswap Lake and Little Shuswap Lake, Seymour, Anstey and Salmon Arms and
Mara Lake**. No stamp is required if you release all such rainbow trout.

### White Sturgeon Conservation Licence

You must purchase a White Sturgeon Conservation Licence, in addition to your basic licence, if you
fish for white sturgeon in the **Fraser River Watershed (including tributaries) from the CPR
Bridge at Mission to and including Williams Lake River in the Cariboo**. This is a
**catch-and-release only** fishery. **This is the only white sturgeon fishery in the province's
non-tidal waters.**

## Classified Waters Licences

> The classified waters of B.C. are highly productive trout streams, classified as either **Class
> I or Class II** and listed in the Water-Specific Tables for each Region.

**Anglers are required to hold a basic angling licence AND a Classified Waters Licence when
fishing on a stream during the period when it is classified.** This is in addition to the basic
licence and any other stamps required by regulation.

A **Steelhead Conservation Surcharge Stamp** is required at all times when fishing for steelhead,
or when fishing classified waters **during the period when steelhead are known to be present**.
The specific times are listed in the Water-Specific Tables for each Region.

| angler | how the Classified Waters Licence works |
|---|---|
| **B.C. resident** | permits angling in **all** classified waters throughout the licence year |
| **NON-GUIDED Non-Resident or Non-Resident Alien** | sold **per day**, **date- and water-specific**; each licence may not exceed **8 consecutive days**. A first-come-first-serve booking system applies to these Kootenay Class II waters: **Michel Creek, Skookumchuck Creek, and the Wigwam River** |
| **GUIDED Non-Resident or Non-Resident Alien** | per day, date- and water-specific; a valid **angling guide number** must be entered; max **8 consecutive days** |

> **EXCEPTION:** Non-Resident Aliens (whether GUIDED or NON-GUIDED) may only purchase **one**
> Classified Waters Licence for the **Dean River** per licence year.

## What this settles

### 1. `angler_class` is confirmed — and it is three axes, not one

The Classified Waters table varies by exactly `residency × guided`, and the age and Indigenous
rules add two more. The full vocabulary:

```
residency:   resident | non_resident | non_resident_alien
guided:      true | false
age:         under_16 | 16_plus
status:      indian_bc_resident | metis | (none)
```

All four are used for real, not hypothetically: a non-guided non-resident alien and a guided one
buy different licences; a BC-resident 16-year-old needs one and a 15-year-old does not; an Indian
resident in B.C. needs none at all; a Métis angler does.

### 2. The steelhead annual quota is PROVINCIAL — this closes an open decision

> *"The annual province-wide quota for hatchery steelhead is 10. All wild steelhead must be
> released."*

`z1`, `z2`, `z3` and `z4` each carry their own `steelhead_annual_quota`, and Region 3's and
Region 4's chapters **do not print it at all** — it was authored into chapters that do not contain
it. It is one provincial rule. The same is true of *"all wild steelhead must be released"*, which
`z1`, `z2`, `z3` and `z6` all restate. **Both move to `region-provincial.json`.**

### 3. `document_required` has a closed document list — 9 values

```
basic_licence · steelhead_stamp · salmon_stamp · kootenay_rainbow_stamp
shuswap_char_stamp · shuswap_rainbow_stamp
white_sturgeon_licence · classified_waters_licence · national_park_permit
```

Plus the **angling guide licence** and **assistant angling guide licence**, which oblige the guide
rather than the angler.

### 4. Three of the five stamps are water-scoped provincial rules

Kootenay Lake rainbow, Shuswap char and Shuswap rainbow are **provincial** rules with a named
water extent — not regional ones. The Shuswap rainbow stamp names seven waters (Shuswap, Little
Shuswap, South Thompson between them, Seymour, Anstey and Salmon Arms, Mara Lake).

**Each is conditional on retention, not on fishing:** *"No stamp is required if you release all…"*
So the condition is `on_retention: true` — a licensing rule gated on a harvest action, which the
catalogue does not currently express.

### 5. The White Sturgeon licence has a precise extent, and an exclusivity claim

Fraser River Watershed including tributaries, **CPR Bridge at Mission to and including Williams
Lake River**. And: *"this is the only white sturgeon fishery in the province's non-tidal waters"* —
which means every other white sturgeon rule in the corpus is a closure, and this one reach is the
sole exception.

### 6. National Parks, again

> *"Basic and supplementary licences and stamps are not valid in National Parks."*

An independent confirmation of the `authority: superior` carve-out — a provincial licence does not
reach into a National Park, so no provincial rule can either.

### 7. Under-16 non-residents share their host's quota

*"Any fish you keep must be counted as part of the catch and possession of your accompanying
licence holder."* That is an **`aggregation_domain` across people**, which the model has no axis
for — quotas aggregate over water, water-group, region and province, but not over anglers.
Recorded as a limitation, not a gap to fill now.
