## General Regulations

**Bait ban:** In the **Skeena River, including tributaries, and Nass River, including tributaries,
year-round.**

**No fishing:** in all rivers and streams for **steelhead, May 15 – June 15**. Exemptions include
mainstem portions of the Skeena, Nass, Iskut, Stikine and Taku Rivers, not currently closed under
the existing winter/spring closure regulation.

**No fishing:** in any stream in the watersheds of the **Skeena River upstream of Cedarvale** or
**Nass River upstream of Kitsault Bridge, Jan 1 – June 15** (NOTE: Nass River mainstem is EXEMPT
and Skeena River mainstem upstream of Cedarvale is only closed **Jan 1 – May 31**).

**No fishing:** in any stream in the **Iskut River watershed (upstream of Forest Kerr Canyon),
Apr 1 – June 30**; and in the **Fraser River watershed in Region 6, Apr 1 – June 30**.

**Single barbless hook:** must be used in all streams of Region 6, all year.

**Set lining:** only permitted in the lakes of Region 6. One line, single hook, gap not less than
3 cm, marked with the angler's name, address and telephone number. **Any game fish that you catch
other than burbot must be released.**

## Region 6 Daily Quotas

```
Arctic grayling: 3        Burbot: 5           Inconnu: 1
Kokanee: 10 (none from streams)               Northern pike: 5
Whitefish: 15 (all species combined)          White sturgeon: CLOSED TO ALL FISHING

Trout/char: 5, but not more than
  • 1 over 50 cm (quota includes hatchery steelhead)
  • 3 Dolly Varden/bull trout and/or lake trout combined
  • 1 trout from streams July 1-Oct 31
And you must release:
  • all Dolly Varden/bull trout from streams all year
  • Trout under 30 cm from any stream
  • Trout of any size from streams, Nov 1-June 30
  • Lake trout from Fraser and Skeena Watersheds, Sept 15-Nov 30
  • all wild steelhead
```

## Possession / Annual

Possession quotas = 2 daily quotas. Annual quota for all B.C.: 10 hatchery steelhead per licence
year. Steelhead Conservation Surcharge Stamp required; Steelhead Stamp mandatory on most Classified
Waters regardless of species. Stop fishing that water once the daily hatchery steelhead quota is
retained.
