The answer, then the details.

v4b, “answer-first, minimal chrome”. The first thing under the bar is open or closed and what you can keep. The bar is one line of context (water, part, day) at 52 px. Everything else is a row you can tap.

Parts

A bottom sheet, every time. Part names are whole sentences (“From the Vedder Crossing Bridge up to the orange triangular boundary signs …”). They don’t fit in pills, and a pill row would mean one pattern for the Vedder and another for the Cowichan’s five parts. Each row has a small map of the whole water with that part drawn in.

Tiles

A list, not a grid. The values are phrases of different lengths (“Single barbless hook”, “Trebles and barbs OK”). In a grid they wrapped unevenly and got cut off. As a list they read top to bottom, and they use the same row as the fish.

One system

Riffle’s tokens, unchanged. There is one card (riffle’s verdict card, used for the answer and the checklist), one chip, one row and one sheet. Status colour is only used for open, closed and release.
