HOW IT WORKS

  • The header is one block, 107 px under the status bar. Name and date pill on one line, the part on the next, a 6 px open-days strip under it. Scroll and it folds to one 48 px line; the strip becomes its bottom edge.
  • Choosing a part. Tap the part line. A panel drops from the header with a small map (every part numbered; tap one) and the same parts as a short list, each with its own open/closed for the day. On the Map tab you can also just tap the other part.
  • Numbers read first. Each fish row is picture · name · conditions in small type · the number, big. Tap a row for the reasons; tap a picture for how to tell it.
  • Under 16 is asked only inside the Licence tile.

WHAT IS CAPTURED

  • Vedder River (2 parts): Jul 1 – Dec 31 on both parts; Jun 1 – 30 (closed) on the mouth part.
  • Kootenay Lake — Lower West Arm: Oct 10 – 11 (weekend) and Oct 12 – 16 (weekdays).
  • Cowichan River: Sep 1 – Nov 15 on the mouth part (closed).
