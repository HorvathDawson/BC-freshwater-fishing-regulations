# Questions the owner will put to BC Fish and Wildlife and DFO

Where the book or DFO's table reads two ways, the app is built on the stricter reading (RULINGS L1, L20, T17) and the
question goes to the office; the looser reading is said after it. An answer goes into RULINGS and its question comes off
this page. Pages are the printed pages of the 2025-2027 synopsis; the traps are `pipeline/docs/GOTCHAS.md`.
Printed errors we found (a wrong unit, a distance that does not fit) are `pipeline/docs/BOOK-ERRORS-FOR-THE-PROVINCE.md`.

Answered by the owner, not asked: Region 6's steelhead closure (p.49, May 15-Jun 15) holds on every stream whose row
prints other "No Fishing" dates (the Kitimat, Dala, Ksi Hlginx, Triumph, and the West Road mainstem in Region 5),
because "no steelhead" is not a full closure. An UNDATED quota under a closure is not on the list: it says nothing about
the season.

**A water's own daily quota against the region's "from streams" share and total** (T834; p.3 says to follow the
provincial, regional and water rules, never how they combine). Built, for every water: the region's cap stands; a
water's number at or below it sits inside it; one above it raises that cap for that fish only.

- Kaslo River, Keen Creek (4, p.37, bull trout 2): 2 from the stream in all. Looser: 2 bull trout plus 2 others.
- Lardeau River, Duncan River (4, p.38, p.36, rainbow 5, bull trout 2): 5 in all, at most 2 of them not rainbow.
  Looser: 7. Under the strict reading a number equal to the share no longer lifts (the 12 rows that carried a hand
  lift: Lardeau, Duncan, Beaver, Lavington, Perry, Whiteswan, Chilliwack, Kitimat ×2, Peace in 7B).
- Kootenay Lake main body (4, p.37, rainbow 10, bull trout 1): 10 in all, others 5 at most. Looser: 11.
- Adams River above Adams Lake (3, p.29, rainbow 2 under "4 from streams"): 4 in all, at most 2 rainbow.
- Buckley Creek (6, p.50, rainbow 1 under "1 trout from streams Jul 1-Oct 31"): 1 trout in all.
- Brook trout 20 lakes (4, p.35-40: Big Fish, Botts, Box, North Star, Premier, Twin, Whitetail) and brook trout 10
  lakes (7, p.59-61; 8, p.70 Jewel): brook trout count toward the region's 5, and the extra slots are brook trout only
  (15 brook trout + 5 cutthroat is legal, 6 cutthroat is not). Stricter: brook trout fill the 5 first. On a raised
  total, what the lifted fish leave on other waters is open, and the card states no number for it.
