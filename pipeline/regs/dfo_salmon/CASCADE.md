# The Region 6 cascade, plainly

Every other region is a flat list: *this river, these dates, this limit.*
**Region 6 is a set of nested boxes.** A rule from the smallest box that contains your
water wins.

## The boxes

```
A  — ALL of Region 6.                      the fallback for anything not covered below
│
├─ B  — the Skeena watershed
│   ├─ B(i)   above the CNR Railway Bridge at Terrace
│   │         └─ named waters: Babine Lake, Babine River, Bulkley, Morice, Kispiox,
│   │            Skeena mainstem, Sustut, Telkwa, Zymoetz …        (18 rivers)
│   └─ B(ii)  below that same bridge
│             └─ named waters: Lakelse, Kitsumkalum, Exchamsiks, Ecstall …  (14)
│
├─ C  — the Nass watershed        └─ Nass, Meziadin, Cranberry, Tseax, Kiteen …  (10)
├─ D  — Haida Gwaii               └─ Yakoun, Pallant, Sheldens, Braverman           (4)
├─ F  — the Fraser watershed part of Region 6   → no salmon fishing, full stop
└─ E  — everything else on the mainland
    ├─ narrower still: streams draining to tidal Area 3/4/5/6
    └─ named waters: Kitimat, Kemano, Dala, Kitlope, Stikine, Taku, Nakina …  (30)
```

**The innermost box is a named river — and often a specific stretch of one.** Babine
Lake alone appears three times, each a different piece of water with different rules.
That is the level almost every actual answer comes from: **210 of Region 6's 235 rules
sit on a named water**, and only 25 on the lettered boxes above them.

## The one rule for reading it

> **Smallest box wins. If the smallest box is silent, ask the box around it.**

The page says so itself, on B, C, D and E:

> *Section "A" applies if stream, specific area, time period, quotas or other species
> restrictions are not listed in the following sections.*

## The full ladder, innermost first

```
1. this stretch of this river   "Babine Lake, excluding tributaries and the mouths of 12 creeks"
2. this river, anywhere         "Babine Lake, including tributaries"
3. this tidal area              "streams flowing into Area 5"        (section E only)
4. this lettered box            "all waters in section B(i)"
5. the box around it            B, then A
```

## Worked example 1 — a named river answers

*Can I keep a coho on the Kispiox River on 1 October?*

1. **Kispiox is listed by name in B(i).** Its own entries cover coho — but the window
   that includes 1 Oct says *no fishing for coho*. → **Done, at step 1.**

## Worked example 2 — the box answers

*Can I keep a chinook on some small unlisted creek in the upper Skeena?*

1. Not listed by name → nothing at step 1 or 2.
2. It is in B(i), so ask B(i)'s catch-all: *no fishing for chinook.* → **That is it.**
3. Had B(i) been silent, you would ask B, then A — which allows 4 chinook a day.

So A's generous "4 chinook per day" almost never applies on the upper Skeena, because a
smaller box says no first. Read flat, the table says the opposite of what it means.

## Worked example 3 — two boxes deep, in section E

*Chinook on a small creek draining into Banks Island (tidal Area 5)?*

1. Not listed by name.
2. Not Skeena, not Nass, not Haida Gwaii, not Fraser → it falls into **E**.
3. E has a narrower box: *streams flowing into tidal water Area 5* — **no fishing for
   chinook.** → Done, before E's own catch-all is ever consulted.

## Why E is not extra work

"Other Mainland Watersheds, **except** the Fraser" sounds like it needs a shape carved
out. It does not:

* a river **listed** in the table already says which box it is in;
* a river **not listed** is placed by asking, in order — Skeena? Nass? Haida Gwaii?
  Fraser? — and if all four say no, it is in E.

E is just *"none of the above"*. Its siblings have to be mapped anyway, so E is free.

## What each box costs to map

| box | how it is drawn |
|---|---|
| A | the Region 6 boundary |
| B, C, F | one river plus everything draining into it |
| B(i), B(ii) | that same Skeena watershed, cut at **one** bridge — one cut, two boxes |
| D | the Haida Gwaii watersheds |
| E | nothing — it is "none of the above" |
| Areas 3/4/5/6 | **the only new machinery**: which tidal area a stream's mouth sits in |

Thirteen boxes to draw, one of which is free, and one mechanism we do not have yet.
