## How a row is decided

- The polygon's candidate is the upstream-most point where the FWA line leaves the polygon (within 75 m of the mouth = the mouth).
- A word source that names a marker is located on the line (a curated split's measure, or an OSM crossing of the named bridge / rail line). Located and within 300 m of the polygon: agree (high). Located and further: the WORDS are proposed and the disagreement is listed (medium).
- Words that say "tidal boundary" without placing it, and streams with no words, take the polygon's clean crossing (medium); a crossing in several pieces or a polygon that misses the stream is low.
- A tidal level gauge (data/curated/gauges/tidal.json, HYDAT remarks) above the polygon's crossing is a disagreement.
- Every proposal is labelled `mouth (tidal boundary)`; one above the FWA mouth also carries the alias `tidal boundary` and the marker's words.
- A curated split within 25 m of the tidal point on the same line (along the line; ground distance for an unresolved splits.json point) is reused: the proposal aliases it `tidal boundary` and accepting it names that split (anchor `split`) instead of a new cut.
