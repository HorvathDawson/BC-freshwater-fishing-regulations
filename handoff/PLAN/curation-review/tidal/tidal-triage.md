## How the edits were found

The unedited DFO original could not be rebuilt: the only copy of the build input (`DFO_TIDAL_BOUNDARY.gpkg` on R2) holds the owner's edited source layer, the DFO ArcGIS host no longer resolves, and the BC WFS does not publish the subareas. So edits are found by a fingerprint, not a diff: DFO's vertices sit on a 1 cm grid in BC Albers; vertices added by hand do not. Runs of off-grid vertices among on-grid ones are edit sites (plus the 2 hand-drawn rows). Blind spots: DFO's own off-grid stretch (south Vancouver Island / Gulf Islands — candidates there are "can't tell"), and edits made only by deleting vertices.

- Edit sites: **68** (3 hand-drawn rings); 18 touch a candidate stream; 0 touch only named streams that are not candidates (listed at the end).
