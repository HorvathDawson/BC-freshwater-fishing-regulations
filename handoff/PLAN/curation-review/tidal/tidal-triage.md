## How the edits were found

The unedited DFO original could not be rebuilt: the only copy of the build input (`DFO_TIDAL_BOUNDARY.gpkg` on R2) holds the owner's edited source layer, the DFO ArcGIS host no longer resolves, and the BC WFS does not publish the subareas. So edits are found by a fingerprint, not a diff: DFO's vertices sit on a 1 cm grid in BC Albers; vertices added by hand do not. Runs of off-grid vertices among on-grid ones are edit sites (plus the 2 hand-drawn rows). The grid is blind in DFO's own off-grid stretch (south Vancouver Island / Gulf Islands) and to edits made only by deleting vertices — the straight-line test below covers both.

Straight-line fingerprint (follow-up 2): DFO's outline follows the shore vertex by vertex, so an edge of 150 m or more with no vertex between that CROSSES an FWA stream is a cut drawn across the channel (kind `straight`). It works inside the off-grid stretch and sees cuts made only by deleting vertices.

- Edit sites: **122** (3 hand-drawn rings, 54 straight cuts across a stream); 33 touch a candidate stream; 0 touch only named streams that are not candidates (listed at the end).
