## Capilano River (owner 2026-10-27: "it should be at the CN railway bridge")

Synopsis 2025-27 p.21 box "Tidal Boundaries on Rivers in Region 2": **"Capilano River: CN Railway Bridge"** (DFO Tidal Waters guide 2015: "Capilano River: BCR Bridge" — the same structure, BC Rail now CN). The only rail line over the Capilano is CN's Squamish Subdivision (OSM way 70954081, operator Canadian National Railway, bridge=yes, 49.32206,-123.14004 to 49.32151,-123.13857). It crosses the FWA line 37 m above the FWA mouth: the river meets Burrard Inlet right under it (OSM's Capilano River way ends on the bridge, the coastline runs just seaward of it). So the proposal IS the bridge — it only looked like "the mouth" because it was labelled "mouth (tidal boundary)" / "at the FWA mouth" and the map opened zoomed out. It is now named "Capilano River: CN Railway Bridge (tidal boundary)" and the map opens on it; the polygon's 1049 m (Marine Drive) stays an alternate.
