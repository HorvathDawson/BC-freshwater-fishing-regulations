  - `✓✓ pre-verified`: two independent methods agree within tolerance.
  - `≠ methods disagree`: the two methods give different answers. The hover says why.
  - `unchecked`: no second method could decide.
- **Filter** by check status. The option labels show counts.
- **"pre-verified first"** order. It is a stable sort, so each queue's own order holds within each group.

- **Entry screen: a new "Split proposals" block** under the pre-checks. For each missing split or ambiguous cut it shows:

## How strong each check is

- **Tidal (DEM):**
  - Negative control: with every proposal moved 1.5 km upstream, only 5 of 1163 still pass, so a pass is meaningful.
  - 631 of the 844 passes are at-mouth points on steep creeks, which is where the DEM is strong.
  - Big rivers mostly come out "unchecked: low gradient". That result is reported as is; the tolerance was not loosened to make them pass.
- **DFO:** each endpoint is checked by a method other than the one that found it:
  - a curated cut matched by name → checked against OSM, GNIS or a lake polygon
  - a split found in OSM → checked against MoTI or GNIS
  - a split found in MoTI → checked against OSM
  - an FWA tributary mouth → checked against the GNIS mouth
  - Tolerances: 150 m for a point, 300 m for a confluence, half the stated distance for an offset.
  - An unnamed nearest feature beyond tolerance counts as "unchecked", not "disagree".
- **Entries:**
  - Ambiguous-cut picks are decided from the rule's words (upstream/downstream of the lake), the curated anchor's inlet or outlet edge, or the other endpoint lying outside the lake's span.
  - New splits are checked by OSM, FWA or GNIS, with the research frozen and cited in `split_suggest.RESEARCH`.
