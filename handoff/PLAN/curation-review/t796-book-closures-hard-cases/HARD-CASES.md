### W1. Campbell River (Region 1): the summer closure does not belong on it (rows 45 and 56)
- **Water:** Campbell River below the Quinsam confluence. Row-45 is 3 sections, pink, Jul 15–Aug 31. Row-56 is 2
  sections, chinook, Jul 15–Aug 31. Sections 58487, 58488 and 58489 are on chains 492 and 493. The DFO places are
  `1:campbell-river:downstream-of-the-confluence-with` and `…~2`.
- **What the app says, Aug 7:** "Rule book" badge, Closed. "Closed to all fishing by the rule book (p.13): Summer
  closure: No Fishing in any stream in Management Units 1-1 to 1-6 from July 15-Aug 31 (see water specific regulations
  table for exceptions). (Jul 15–Aug 31)". The source line reads "Rule book p.13 · Streams of MUs 1-1 to 1-6". Chain
  492 shows this text Jul 15–31. From Aug 1 it shows Campbell r3 (Maple Street stretch, Aug 1–Oct 31), which is correct.
- **What the book says:** The table lists the river as **"CAMPBELL RIVER 1-10"** (printed p.14). MU 1-10 is not in
  1-1 to 1-6. The river is the boundary between 1-6 and 1-10: both MU polygons cover each section (`where.py`:
  1-10 = 0.97–1.0, 1-6 = 0.94–0.97 of the length), and the bundle gives `mu_set` "1-10,1-6". Two more pieces of
  evidence against the closure. First, the Campbell row's own "No Fishing … Maple Street … cement block, Aug 1-Oct 31"
  would mean nothing in August if the summer closure already closed the whole river. Second, the Quinsam (1-6, which
  joins here) needs and prints "Exempt from July 15-Aug 31 summer closure", while the Campbell (1-10) prints no
  exemption.
- **Effect:** The app closes pink and chinook on the lower Campbell from Jul 15 to Aug 31, where DFO opens them. The
  trout card has the same reading, since the bundle binds the summer closure to every section with a 1-6 code. The
  summer closure is also on the Campbell's upper sections (words only).
- **Fix direction (pipeline):** For a stream in an MU-scoped zone rule, a water whose table row names its MU (1-10)
  should follow that MU, not a boundary-straddling `mu_set`. Owner call.
- **Re-check:** `python3 -I geo.py row-45 row-56` (mus). With the repo venv: `python -I where.py row-45 row-56` (MU
  polygons). `python3 -I show.py row-45 Aug`. For the book, `python -I crop_name.py 16 "CAMPBELL RIVER" x.png 70`.

### W2. An all-year book closure loses its dates when another book place answers on some days (rows 15, 22, 37, 46, 52, 58)
- **Mechanism (core `bookToday`, `salmon.ts` ~l.468):** The card adds "(all year)" only when that place answers
  (`a`) for the species on EVERY day. When a chain holds two book places, the first one sorted by id answers on its own
  days and the all-year one answers on the rest. On those other days the words carry **no dates at all**, because the
  pipeline's verdict for an all-year rule has no `when`.
- **Cases (offline and under the live feed, every day it answers):**
  - row-15, chain 881 (75 sections): the Stikine reserve. The reserve answers Jul 1–Mar 31 because iskut_fraser r1
    takes Apr 1–Jun 30. Words: "…(p.9): Fishing is prohibited in Ecological Reserves in B.C." with no dates.
  - row-22, chain 568 (Statlu Creek, 198 sections): "…(p.26): No Fishing". No dates and no water in the line (the
    source line names Statlu Creek). Chehalis r2 takes May.
  - row-37, chain 622 (Fergus Creek, 9 sections): "…(p.23): No Fishing", Jul 1–Mar 31. Campbell (R2) r2 takes
    Apr 1–Jun 30 and names "Rule book p.22 · Campbell River" on Fergus Creek. That is correct, since the Campbell row
    carries ✱, but it can surprise.
  - row-46, chain 948 (Morice reserve, 3 sections): reserve words without dates Jun 16–Dec 31. skeena_nass r1 takes
    Jan 1–Jun 15.
  - row-52, chain 873 (Chuckwalla Ten Mile Pool, 2 sections): "…(p.44): No Fishing between fishing boundary signs at Ten
    Mile Pool" without dates, Sep 1–May 31. r1 takes Jun 1–Aug 31.
  - row-58, chain 487 (Chemainus Copper Canyon, 1 section): r3 without dates Oct 1–Nov 30. r2 takes Dec 1–Sep 30.
- **The book:** each rule is all year, so the owner's pass condition ("the dates") calls for "(all year)".
- **Fix direction:** decide "all year" from the place's own year (`payload.static[lid]`, every day ≥ 0), not from `a`.
- **Re-check:** `vite-node render.ts`, then grep `out/render-fail.json` for "book words carry no dates". Expect 0.
