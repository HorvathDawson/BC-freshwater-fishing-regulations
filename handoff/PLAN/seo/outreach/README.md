# Outreach kit (T197) — drafts only, the owner sends every message by hand

Plan: `../PLAN.md` section 7. Nothing here is sent, scheduled or posted by software.

## The rules these drafts keep
- One honest message each, written and sent by a person: it is a free, non-profit app the owner made, and it asks for
  **feedback**, never for links, shares, votes or reviews.
- No scripts, no repeated posting, no sockpuppets, no incentivised votes, no purchased or traded links, no comment or
  forum auto-posting. Each group is contacted once; a reply gets a reply; nothing is re-sent.
- Read each group's own rules first. Where self-promotion is not allowed, do not post; ask a moderator instead.
- Never ask anyone to link to the site. If a club chooses to, that is theirs to decide.
- A reported wrong rule gets a reply and a fix (that is the best word of mouth).
- Fill the `[brackets]` before sending. Do not claim anything the app does not do. The app is not a legal source; every
  message says the provincial synopsis stays the authority.

## Files
- `club-email.md` — angling clubs, conservation and stewardship groups, rod-and-gun associations
- `ffsbc-email.md` — the Freshwater Fisheries Society of BC and fish-and-game associations
- `forum-post.md` — forums and Facebook groups where self-promotion is allowed
- `reddit-post.md` — one maker post for r/bcfishing
- `shop-counter-card.md` — tackle shops and guides: a counter card with a QR code, and the region page to put on their site
- `tracker.csv` — who was contacted, when, what they said (one row per contact; fill by hand)

Region pages for shops and clubs: `https://canifishthis.ca/region/<1-8>`. A water page: `https://canifishthis.ca/water/<slug>`.
