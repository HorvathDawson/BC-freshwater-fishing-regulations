# Draft: email to the Freshwater Fisheries Society of BC, or a fish-and-game association

Send by hand, once, to the organisation's published contact.

**Subject:** Free regulations companion for BC anglers — feedback welcome

Hello [name],

I'm [owner name]. I made a free, non-profit app, Can I Fish This?, that shows anglers whether a BC freshwater water is open
today and what they can keep, in plain words. Every rule comes from the provincial Freshwater Fishing Regulations Synopsis
and cites its printed page; the app says clearly that it is not a legal source and sends people to the synopsis.

It is at https://canifishthis.ca. I'm not asking for anything beyond your feedback: is there anything in how it presents the
rules, or your stocking and fishery information, that you would want changed or left out? I'd also like to know if there is
a better way to credit your data than the way I do now (About page: https://canifishthis.ca/about), and I will respect
whatever terms of use you set.

If it ever seems useful to your anglers, you are welcome to say so, and equally welcome not to. Either way, thank you for
the work you do.

[owner name]
[contact]
