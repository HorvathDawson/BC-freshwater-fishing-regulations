# Draft: counter card for tackle shops and guides

Hand it over in person with a short chat. Print on one A6 card. The QR code goes to the shop's own region page:
`https://canifishthis.ca/region/[n]` (made by the owner by hand with any QR tool; check it scans before printing).

---

**Is it open? Ask your phone.**

Can I Fish This? — a free, non-profit app for BC freshwater anglers.
Look up a water: open today or not, and what you can keep, in plain words, with the synopsis page for each rule.

[QR code → https://canifishthis.ca/region/[n]]

Not a legal document. Always check the BC Freshwater Fishing Regulations Synopsis.
Spot a mistake? Tell [owner name] at [contact]: it gets fixed.

---

What to say to the shop (feedback, not links): "I made this free app. Would you try it on a few waters you get asked about
and tell me if anything is wrong? If it's useful, the card is yours to keep at the counter; if not, no hard feelings."
The shop's region page (`/region/[n]`) is theirs to put on their own site if they choose; do not ask them to.
