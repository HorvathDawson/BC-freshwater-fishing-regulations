# Draft: email to an angling club or conservation group

Send by hand, from the owner's own address, to the club's published contact. One message per club.

**Subject:** A free BC regulations app I made — would you take a look?

Hi [name],

I'm [owner name], an angler in [town]. I've made a free, non-profit app called Can I Fish This? that tells you whether a
BC freshwater water is open today and what you can keep, in plain words, for [region / the waters your members fish].
It reads the provincial Freshwater Fishing Regulations Synopsis and cites the printed page for each rule. It is not a
legal source, so it always points people back to the synopsis.

It costs nothing, has no ads and asks for no account. You can look up any water at https://canifishthis.ca (for example
https://canifishthis.ca/region/[n] for [region]).

I'd really value your feedback: does it match how the rules read to your members, and is anything on your home waters
wrong or confusing? If you spot a mistake, tell me and I'll fix it and reply.

Thanks for the work the club does for local fish and water.

[owner name]
[contact]

---
Notes for the sender: do not ask for a link, a mention or a newsletter slot. If they offer, say thanks. Log the contact in
`tracker.csv`. Do not follow up more than once, and only if they asked a question.
