## Part 2 — palette D (applied 2026-10-22)

**What D keeps from B:** one neutral grey boundary per theme (light `#7F7C79`, dark `#B8B8B8`, cvd `#8B857F`), the unit
boundary an alias of it (`@color.region.edge`), region 3 px, unit dotted `[1, 2.5]`, light lakes at `opacity.lake` 0.6.

**What D changes: all five status colours, chosen together.** A to C were judged on closed / own / base; restricted (the
permit-area hatch, drawn over every water) and open (the cool temperature dot beside the warm = restricted and critical =
closed ones) stayed where they were, 6–9 apart from their neighbours under deuteranopia. D moves every one of them.

Lightness AND hue: in light, restricted is the darkest (L\* ≈ 4), then closed, open, base, own the lightest; in dark the
order flips with the ground. Open left the mid green: to a protan or deutan reader a mid green IS a dark red and IS a mid
grey (open vs closed 7.8, open vs the grey boundary 14.9), and open is never water — it is a temperature dot and a card
word — so it became the cool colour it stands for. The colour-blind palette takes the light statuses (D was chosen under
all three simulations already) except open, which is a deeper blue there because the cvd theme's mapped-water teal
`#0B6987` sits CIE76 6.4 from the light cerulean (build-style's house floor); its open and base are no longer one blue
(part 1, question 5).

**How it was found.** `scripts/search-d.mjs <theme>`: random sampling inside each status's hue family (LCh), then hill
climbing on a smooth minimum, maximising the WORST ΔE2000 over every adjacent pair (`isMapPair`) as tokens, thin lines on
every ground, lake fills on the basemap's water, and thin lines over satellite (held 3 below the basemap grounds), plus
every status against B's boundary grey (token +2, line +6) and against its own grounds (+5) — normal vision and protan /
deutan / tritan under Viénot and Machado. Hard rules (a candidate breaking one is discarded): every app gate as it stands —

base off the flavour's grounds, the cvd gate's red-green 20 / tritan 18 — plus build-style's house floor (CIE76 12 from
every map token some view draws with it, by build-style's own "together" relation), every two statuses ≥ 20 apart in
normal vision (one meaning per colour, non-adjacent pairs included) and ≥ 15 from body ink and the accent, and the
boundary held at least where B had it (token 15, line 5.5). Dark also holds the owner's closed/own pair as a thin line at
least where it was before T47 (19.6). Reproduce (`scripts/search-d.mjs`, env `T47_BNDL_MIN=5.5 T47_ITER=9000

## What the measurement says (today's palette)

1. **The region boundary is a closure colour.** Light theme, region `#8E2F52` against closed `#940018`: ΔE 10.4 as tokens
   (tritan), and **0.5** when both are drawn as thin lines over a river. Dark: region `#E29AB4` vs closed 19.8; the unit
   boundary `#A6947A` vs closed 7.4 (deutan) and 1.8 over snow on satellite. The owner's "region red reads as closed" is
   measurably true, for every reader.
2. **Own vs closed passes the existing gate as tokens and fails as drawn.** `tools/water-status-cvd.test.ts` holds the
   tokens ≥ 20 apart and today's light pair is 22.5 (deutan). Drawn as a 1–2 px antialiased line the pair drops to 18.9,
   as lake fills (light `opacity.lake` 0.4 over the basemap's water) to **17.0** (tritan), over satellite to 14.2; the
   light closed/base lakes are 14.7 (deutan). The tritan column matters
   for everyone, not only the 0.01 % who are tritanopes: on a thin line, normal vision loses most of its blue–yellow
   discrimination (small-field tritanopia), and under tritan simulation own `#B37B00` becomes a dusty pink `#b77474` and
   closed `#940018` a red `#940404`. Both read "dark warm red-brown" — the owner's complaint.
3. **The colour-blind palette fails the owner's pair worst of all.** cvd theme closed `#C80D00` vs own `#B37B00`: **8.6**
   (deutan) as tokens, 9.1 as lines. No gate holds the cvd theme to the map's status pairs: `tools/cvd.test.ts` checks
   closed / restricted / open only, and `water-status-cvd.test.ts` checks light and dark only.
4. Card words: every status clears 3:1 on the card today (own light 3.65, the lowest). Pills (closed / restricted /
   open on their own tint) all clear 4.5:1.
5. Pairs outside the map's three (reported, not the ticket's subject): light closed vs restricted 9.0 (deutan) and
   restricted vs open 8.9 (protan) on the card; the cvd theme's open `#0063D2` and base `#0072B2` are 1.1 apart
   (two meanings, one blue — never on the same surface today, but the rule "one colour per meaning" is broken).

## How it was measured

- ΔE2000 with the app's own maths (`app/tools/cvd.ts`, imported by `scripts/colour-math.mjs`): every number is the WORST
  of normal vision and protan / deutan / tritan under both Viénot 1999 and Machado 2009, the rule
  `water-status-cvd.test.ts` already uses.
- "As drawn": a thin line's pixels are its colour over the ground at partial coverage (0.6 for a status line, 0.8 for a
  boundary, which is wider); both lines of a pair sit on the same ground, and the worst ground counts. Lakes: the fill at
  `opacity.lake` over the basemap's WATER, which the status lake layer is drawn on top of. Pinned against the renders
  (`scripts/colour-math.test.mjs`): Kootenay Lake in today's light z9 shot is `#94aa86`, which is own `#B37B00` at 0.4
  over the river blue `#82cdd9`; over land the model would say `#c39b7a`. The grounds were sampled from the baseline
  screenshots (table 4), not taken from the basemap flavour (the app's basemap is its own); a status-coloured sample
  (Cultus Lake's own fill) was dropped from them.
- Which pairs are held to a floor: closed / own / base against each other (lines and lakes); restricted, the
  permit-area colour, against each of them; the temperature trio open / restricted / closed; and BOTH boundaries against
  ALL FIVE statuses (`isMapPair` in `scripts/colour-math.mjs`).
- Card: WCAG contrast on `color.ui.card`, and as pill text on a 13.3 % tint of itself (as `tools/cvd.test.ts`).

const THEMES = ["light", "dark", "cvd"] as const;
/** Every status a boundary can cross or run beside: water lines and lakes, permit areas, temperature dots. */
const ALL_STATUSES = ["closed", "own", "base", "restricted", "open"] as const;
/**
 * The grounds a LINE is drawn on, sampled from the T47 baseline screenshots (handoff/PLAN/app-data/status-colours/
 * scripts/colour-math.mjs GROUNDS): land, forest, river, meadow. No status-coloured pixel is a ground.
 */
const GROUND: Record<string, string[]> = {
  light: ["#d9cecc", "#b0caa8", "#82cdd9", "#e9e7e1"],
  dark: ["#372e2f", "#221e1e", "#31353f"],
};
GROUND.cvd = GROUND.light;            // the colour-blind palette has a light ground only
/**
 * The ground under a status LAKE: the basemap's own water polygon, which the status lake layer is drawn on top of
 * (Kootenay Lake in the light z9 render is #94aa86 = own #B37B00 at 0.4 over #82cdd9).
 */
const LAKE_GROUND: Record<string, string> = { light: "#82cdd9", dark: "#31353f" };
LAKE_GROUND.cvd = LAKE_GROUND.light;
/** Mean coverage of a 1-2 px line's pixels; a boundary is wider (2.4 / 1.2 px) and dashed, so it covers more. */
const LINE = 0.6, EDGE = 0.8;

const STATUS_FLOOR = 20;    // tokens, the existing floor, now in the cvd theme too
const LINE_FLOOR = 18;      // two thin status lines on the same ground
const LAKE_FLOOR = 15;      // two lake fills at opacity.lake, over the basemap's water
const BOUNDARY_FLOOR = 20;  // a boundary token against ANY status token: the ticket's number, every theme
const BOUNDARY_LINE_FLOOR = 10;

const over = (fill: string, under: string, a: number) => {
  const [f, u] = [parseHex(fill), parseHex(under)];
  return `#${f.map((c, i) => Math.round(c * a + u[i]! * (1 - a)).toString(16).padStart(2, "0")).join("")}`;
};
const worst = (a: string, b: string) => worstSeparation(a, b, MODELS);
/** The worst separation of a and b drawn at coverage ca / cb over every ground. */
const drawn = (a: string, b: string, grounds: string[], ca: number, cb: number) =>
  grounds.map((g) => ({ g, ...worst(over(a, g, ca), over(b, g, cb)) })).sort((x, y) => x.dE - y.dE)[0]!;

describe("status colours, as drawn", () => {
  for (const th of THEMES) {
    const t = resolveTheme(th) as Record<string, string | number>;
    const colour = (s: string) => t[`color.status.${s}`] as string;

    it(`keeps every pair of map statuses apart as tokens, thin lines and lake fills — ${th}`, () => {
      const lake = [LAKE_GROUND[th]!];   // a status lake lies on the basemap's water
      for (let i = 0; i < WATER_STATUSES.length; i++)
        for (let j = i + 1; j < WATER_STATUSES.length; j++) {
          const [a, b] = [colour(WATER_STATUSES[i]!), colour(WATER_STATUSES[j]!)];
          const name = `${th}: ${WATER_STATUSES[i]} ${a} vs ${WATER_STATUSES[j]} ${b}`;
          const p = worst(a, b);
          expect(p.dE, `${name} as tokens, under ${p.under}`).toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(STATUS_FLOOR);
          const l = drawn(a, b, GROUND[th]!, LINE, LINE);
          expect(l.dE, `${name} as thin lines on ${l.g}, under ${l.under}`).toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(LINE_FLOOR);
          const k = drawn(a, b, lake, t["opacity.lake"] as number, t["opacity.lake"] as number);
          expect(k.dE, `${name} as lake fills on water, under ${k.under}`).toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(LAKE_FLOOR);
        }
    });

    it(`never lets a boundary read as a status — ${th}`, () => {
      for (const b of ["color.region.edge", "color.mu.edge"])
        for (const s of ALL_STATUSES) {
          const [bc, sc] = [t[b] as string, colour(s)];
          const p = worst(bc, sc);
          expect(p.dE, `${th}: ${b} ${bc} vs ${s} ${sc}, under ${p.under}`).toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(BOUNDARY_FLOOR);
          const l = drawn(bc, sc, GROUND[th]!, EDGE, LINE);
          expect(l.dE, `${th}: ${b} vs ${s} as lines on ${l.g}, under ${l.under}`)
            .toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(BOUNDARY_LINE_FLOOR);
        }
    });

    it(`draws the boundaries neutral: grey or ink, never a status hue — ${th}`, () => {
      for (const b of ["color.region.edge", "color.mu.edge"]) {
        const [r, g, bl] = parseHex(t[b] as string);
        expect(Math.max(r!, g!, bl!) - Math.min(r!, g!, bl!), `${th}: ${b} ${t[b]}`).toBeLessThanOrEqual(16);
      }
    });
  }

  it("tells the region from the unit by width and dash, never by colour alone", () => {
    for (const th of THEMES) {
      const t = resolveTheme(th) as Record<string, unknown>;
      expect(t["width.region.edge"] as number, th).toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(2 * (t["width.admin.edge"] as number));
      expect(Array.isArray(t["dash.mu"]) && (t["dash.mu"] as number[]).length >= 2, th).toBe(true);
    }
  });
});
