Search in the top bar

The browse maps get a top bar — menu · an always-open search field · the reserved sync slot — with Layers and Spots on the side rail. Search and Spots leave the bottom bar, so it is down to Map and Conditions: pick one of the three bottom options below before T48 is built. Then the search transition (tap the field → it focuses in place → the map dims → the results sheet rises over the same map; tap a place → the sheet lowers to half height and the map flies there with its nearby waters lit), a reduced-motion variant, and the placeholder lines.

Drawn at 390×844 in the app's own tokens (app/packages/map/style/themes/{light,dark}.json, color.ui.* / color.status.* / color.highlight), its type scale (type.ts: Bricolage Grotesque, Archivo, JetBrains Mono), and its components' shapes (TabBar, MapRail, Pill, LegendStrip, MapDayButton, SearchScreen's rows, Sheet). Square corners and offset slabs, as the app. The map ground is the app's own web screenshot of the Fraser valley (committed under handoff/PLAN/app-data/map-chrome/), with the old controls cropped or patched away; the lit traces over it are hand-placed for the mock. The status bar, home indicator and keyboard are the phone's, drawn only to show what they cover.

(a) The bottom bar, once Search and Spots have left it

Each option on the Map tab (light and dark) and on Conditions. The top bar and the rail are the same in all three; only where Map / Conditions is chosen differs. The legend strip (the day button and the three statuses, or the flow ramp) stays at the bottom in every option.

Map height between the top bar and the legend (measured below):

Option 1

A two-item bottom bar: Map · Conditions

Today's TabBar with Search gone: the same printed ink rule, icons and underscored label, two items. The legend strip stays where it is, above the bar.

For

  • Nothing new to learn — the bar every user has seen, with one item fewer.
  • Thumb-reach switch at the bottom; the screen reader keeps its tablist.
  • Water mode already hides the bar (D1), so the rule "bar on browse, gone on a water" is unchanged.

Against

  • Two items is a thin bar: 96 px of the screen (62 + the 34 px home-indicator inset) for one binary switch.
  • The least map of the three: 568 px clear between the top bar and the legend, against 601 (2a) and 641 (2b).
  • Two tab bars of two items read as a toggle wearing a tab bar's clothes.
Option 2a

No bottom bar: the switch IN the top bar

The top bar grows a second row: a two-segment Map / Conditions strip in the app's segmented style (the LayersSheet / Sheet segmented control: 1.5 ink edge, the chosen one filled ink). The rail drops under it.

For

  • Every map control in one block at the top; the bottom is map, legend, and nothing else.
  • +33 px of clear map against option 1 (601 vs 568); the strip floats over the map, so the map itself is +73.
  • The switch reads as a mode of the one map, which is what it is (same map, different colouring).

Against

  • The top block is taller (34 px more) and the rail starts lower.
  • Far from the thumb on a tall phone.
  • The segment row is 34 px tall: it needs its hitSlop to make a 44 px target.
Option 2b

No bottom bar: a compact switch UNDER the top bar

A small two-chip switch floats under the bar's left end, in the horizon chips' style (the chosen one filled accent, like "Now"). On Conditions the horizon chips take the row under it.

For

  • The lightest chrome: the switch is the size of what it does.
  • +73 px of map against option 1 (641 vs 568); the rail keeps its place under the bar.
  • Same visual language as the horizon chips, so it reads as "a choice about this map".

Against

  • On Conditions there are two rows of floating chips at the top-left (switch, then horizons).
  • Accent fill is already "you chose this" for the horizon chip right under it: two filled chips stacked.
  • Smallest target of the three (34 px tall, needs hitSlop).

Questions for the owner

  1. Which bottom bar? Option 1 (two-item bar Map · Conditions), 2a (a Map / Conditions strip inside the top bar), or 2b (a compact switch under the top bar). Clear map between the top chrome and the legend: 568 px (1), 601 px (2a), 641 px (2b).
  2. Long placeholder lines are cut. At 390 wide with the sync slot drawn, the lines marked “Cut” in (c) do not fit. Shorten them (e.g. “Cast for a lake or river”, “Drop a line: river or lake”), or let the field take the sync slot's width until sync exists? (Without the slot both fit, the first with no room to spare: 236 of 236 px.)
  3. The empty sync slot — keep a 44 px gap (the field never changes width when sync arrives), or let the field fill it until then? The mocks draw it as a dashed outline only so it can be seen; the default would draw nothing there.
  4. How search closes. The mocks turn the menu button into ‹ while searching (same box, same place). Alternatively a “Cancel” word at the right. Back / Escape close it either way.
  5. The eye goes. A row tap shows (frames the map); the selected row's › opens the water through openWater(). Is a › in an accent box the right “open” mark, or should it read “Open”?
  6. The half sheet covers the bottom bar and the legend while a place is shown (F6/F7). Alternatively the half sheet stops above the bar (option 1 only) — 96 px less map for the place.
  7. Reduced motion, camera change (R4): a cross-fade of the camera needs a snapshot of the old map over the new one (one MapLibre map can't draw two cameras). Accept a plain cut of the camera under the sheet's fade instead?
  8. 360-wide phones, Conditions: the five horizon chips (Now … +10d) fit beside the rail at 390 (304 px of chips, 18 px clear) but run under the rail at 360. Wrap them, or move the rail down on Conditions?
