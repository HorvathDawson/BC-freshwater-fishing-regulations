# Horizons: the map control is back, and +10 days is added

Owner, 2026-10-20: "the condition 1 3 5 day forecast is gone in condition map?" and "also add 10 day to it".

## What changed

1. **The map's horizon control is back** on the Conditions map, top-left: Now · +1d · +3d · +5d · +10d. P16's review
   fix (2d784837) had removed it. It works as it did before, with two differences:
   - **One state, two controls.** `useHorizon()` in `@app/ui` holds the horizon. The Shell gives its `chips` and
     `pick` to the map's corner and its `horizon` and `pick` to the panel header. Both draw `horizonChips` (core's
     `HORIZONS` and `HORIZON_LABELS`), so the two controls cannot list different days, word them differently, or
     disagree about which day is chosen. The map is replaced by the Conditions panel while a section is open, so the
     two are never on screen together.
   - **No reset to Now when the panel closes.** P16 added that reset because the map had no control showing which
     horizon it was painting. It has one again, and it shows the choice made in the panel.
   - Under Temp the map control is hidden (`mapModes.horizonsShown`), as before. Temperature has no forecast.
2. **A 10-day horizon, end to end.**
   - Core: `HORIZONS = [0, 1, 3, 5, 10]`. The words are derived: "+10d", "FORECAST · 10 DAYS AHEAD", "Forecast 10 days
     ahead".
   - Publisher: `HORIZONS = (1, 3, 5, 10)`. +10 is ranked against the envelope for that day, the same way +1/+3/+5
     are. Each horizon is answered by the first model that reaches it (CLEVER, then ELF).
   - **Half a step of slack** in `forecast_at` (capped at the old 18 h). CLEVER's run ends at 23:00 on day nine of its
     issue date, so a publish early the next morning asks for +10 a few hours past that end. The flat 18 h slack let
     the run's last hour answer for day ten. Now ELF answers instead. This makes no difference inside a run.
   - The app reads `ahead` **by day key** (`row.ahead[String(day)]`), never by position.
   - **No run reaches +10** → the publisher leaves `"10"` out (the existing convention: absent, not null). The app
     reads that as null. The panel says the new reason **`no-forecast`**: "No forecast for the gauges here reaches this
     far ahead." It does not say "no gauge close enough", which would be false, and it never shows the +5 value. The
     map draws no reading there. The panel keeps its chips on this refusal so the reader can step back. Before this
     change, a refusal hid the chips, which would have stranded a reader on +10.
   - The dev fixture's `index.json` carries "10" (regenerated by `build-fixture.mjs`, byte-identical to the hand
     patch). `feed-contract.test.ts` checks the keys against core's `HORIZONS`.

## Verified live

I ran the publisher once into the worktree's `scratch-feed/` (not committed), using the main checkout's roster and
`clim.json` read-only:

- 425/439 stations answered.
- 363 stations have +1/+3/+5/+10, all answered at +10 by ELF. 8 have +1/+3/+5 only, and the app says `no-forecast`
  for them at +10. 54 have no forecast.

A throwaway proxy served `/feeds/live/*` from that directory and passed everything else through, read-only, to the
owner's `:39217`. The web app ran on its own port with `EXPO_PUBLIC_DATA_BASE` pointing at the proxy. Screenshots in
this folder:

- `conditions-now.png`
- `conditions-plus10.png` (the Fraser repaints from normal to high)
- `conditions-plus5.png`

All my servers are stopped. The owner's `:39217` and `:39218` were never touched.

**Worth knowing:** +10 is usually ELF and +5 is usually CLEVER. A station can therefore jump between +5 and +10
because the model changed, not because the river did. In the live run, 07EA005 went from p5 to p42. ELF also
publishes level, so under Level the map colours more water at +10 than at +5.
