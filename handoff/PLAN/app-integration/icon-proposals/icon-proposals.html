Icon proposals

Five launcher directions and one new set of map marks, for the owner to pick from. Nothing here is wired into the app. The colours are the app's own theme tokens. Launcher art sits on Android's 108 adaptive canvas: the launcher shows the central 72, and the art stays inside the 66-diameter safe circle, so a circle, squircle or square mask never cuts it. The SVGs are in handoff/PLAN/app-integration/icon-proposals/, made by build.mjs.

2 · Map marks: fish obstacles

The per-type map marks are the fish obstacles (T173, owner 2026-10-28: "small, nice icons per type"). They are drawn from one table, @app/core obstacleIcons.ts OBSTACLE_ICON_SHAPES (a 16 grid), rasterised per theme by @app/map obstacle-icons.ts (3x, signed-distance AA, addImage / native Images), and named in the style by layers.source.json layer obstacle (icon.images by type, emitted by tools/build-style.mjs). The tap sheet and Layers key draw the same table (ObstacleIcon in ui-native icons.tsx). The other point marks are single-look marks, not per-type: gauge dot/pill (gaugeDots.ts), saved spot and parking square (spot-marks.ts), boundary marks, tap marker and pins (point-marks.ts).

The proposed set. The grid stays at 16 (live area 2–14) and the ink and halo stay neutral (color.obstacle.mark / .ring), so the rule that colour on this map is an answer still holds. Changes:

  • One stroke: 2.0 with square caps and miter joins, where the current set mixes 1.4, 1.7 and 2.
  • Two tiers by shape. A full barrier (dam, falls) is a solid ink plate with the glyph cut out. A partial barrier (canyon, cascade, velocity, culvert) is the bare glyph. The tiers follow the existing sortOrder, and you can tell them apart without reading the glyph.
  • Unknown becomes a diamond. A dot is a gauge on this map.
  • Halo 1.5. It is up from 1.4, so the mark holds on imagery.

Why not Lucide's look (round caps, stroke 1.75). At 14 px under a halo, round caps and joins blur into blobs: the cascade's steps and the chevrons' points go soft. Square ends match the app's square corners and stay crisp when a 3x raster is shown at 1x. A filled plate is also the only thing that stays visible over busy satellite texture.

Wiring a chosen set in (not done)

  • Launcher: app/tools/build-icons.mjs composes everything from one favicon.svg. A chosen direction has separate background and foreground layers, so the script would read background.svg, foreground.svg and monochrome.svg instead of recolouring one drawing. It then writes icon-1024, play-512, adaptive-foreground, adaptive-monochrome, favicon-48 and the feature graphic. app.config.js sets the adaptive background colour from color.ui.card, so it would need the direction's ground instead, or a background image. The web favicon (favicon.svg, which is also the site's) changes with it.
  • Markers: only data. Replace OBSTACLE_ICON_SHAPES in app/packages/core/src/obstacleIcons.ts. The rasteriser in app/packages/map/src/obstacle-icons.ts needs a "plate" shape kind, an inverted knockout, and a square-cap/miter stroke distance; today strokes are round by construction. Also update OBSTACLE_ICON_HALO and the ObstacleIcon SVG in ui-native (via obstacleIconSvg), and re-pin obstacleIcons.test.ts / obstacle-icons.test.ts. No tile, style or sprite change: the image names stay obstacle-<type>.
