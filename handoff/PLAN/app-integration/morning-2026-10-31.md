`trackUserLocation` does not follow, though our own `locate` (expo-location) gets the fix. Not a small, clearly safe fix
without device iteration, so stopped per the brief. Lead: does MLRN 11's native location engine start (permission was
granted while the app ran; it also needs its own updates — check logcat for MLRN location lines), or does it need

from `me.fix` (a ShapeSource circle, like the web's DOM dot) and follow with `easeTo` as the web does.

`.venv/bin/python deploy/staging/play-upload.py --package ca.canifishthis.app --max-version-code` should print 6.
