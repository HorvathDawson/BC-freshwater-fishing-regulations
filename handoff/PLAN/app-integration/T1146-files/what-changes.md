# T1146: what changes for anglers (56 sections, 47.7 km, gained only, nothing lost)

- **Koksilah River:** its side channels near the mouth (5 sections, 6.2 km) and 4 small creeks feeding them (3.8 km) are now closed to fishing Jul 15-Aug 31, like the rest of the Cowichan system (Cowichan River, including tributaries). They also get the DFO salmon notice "Cowichan River, including tributaries".
- **Forsyth Creek:** its side channel (1.0 km) now has the Elk River tributaries rules: no fishing Sep 1-Oct 31; trout/char quota 1 (none under 30 cm) Jun 15-Aug 31; bait ban Jun 15-Aug 31; bull trout catch and release.
- **Shelagyote River:** its side channel (0.1 km) and 19 small creeks feeding it (19.0 km) now have the Babine and Skeena bait bans, the Babine's "no angling from boats" rule, and Class I water Sep 1-Oct 31 (Babine River unit).
- **MacMillan Creek:** its side channel to Diana Lake (0.8 km) now has the Skeena River bait ban.
- **Halfway River:** 9 side channels (3.7 km) and 3 small creeks feeding them (2.2 km) now have Arctic grayling and mountain whitefish catch and release (Peace River, including tributaries).
- **Manson River:** 3 side-channel pieces (3.5 km) and 3 creeks feeding them (2.1 km) now have Arctic grayling catch and release (Williston Lake tributaries).
- **Omineca River:** its side channel (2.4 km) and 5 creek pieces feeding it (2.6 km) now have the same grayling rule.
- **Old Kootenay River Channel:** its last 170 m piece, into a pond in the Creston Valley WMA, now has burbot catch and release, which the channel's other 15 km already had.
- **No change on the Nechako.** A 735 m channel lies in Region 7A, and the Fraser-watershed DFO closure it was predicted to gain applies to Region 6 only.
- **Every other water in the province is unchanged:** no rule or place loses a section.
- **Parts that go away:** Forsyth p1, Koksilah p1, MacMillan p2, Shelagyote p0, Omineca p0 and Manson p0 join the river part beside them. Halfway p2 shrinks from 12 to 3 sections. The other 19 of the 26 nameless parts are unchanged.
