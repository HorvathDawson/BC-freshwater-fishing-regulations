Push token
On an iPhone, when notifications are on and at least one is set (a gauge alert, “New regulations data” or “New app version”), the app sends my server a push token for this phone: an address Apple issues so a message can reach the app. With it I keep only whether it is for Apple’s test or live push service and when it was registered. I use it only to send, through Apple’s push service, a silent signal telling the app to check for new data; the signal carries no words, no waters and no location. I do not receive your alerts, the waters you watch or where you are. I do not store your IP address with it: to limit abuse I keep only a salted one-way hash of it for a day’s counting. The token is deleted when you turn notifications off, or when Apple tells me it no longer works. It is not linked to you or to an account and is not backed up.

The short version (second item):
The app sends me three things: a problem report, only when you press Send; unless you switch it off, a short error report when the app itself fails; and, on an iPhone with notifications on, a push token so I can tell the app to check for new data.

No selling, no sharing (second sentence):
The services named above (Cloudflare, Open-Meteo, Esri, and Apple for push only) receive only the requests described here.
