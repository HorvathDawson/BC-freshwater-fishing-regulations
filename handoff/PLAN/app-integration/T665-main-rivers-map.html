Held rivers: tick each one

Each card is one river or creek the T665 rule would cut at the DFO tidal polygon. It is held as it is today until you tick it. The orange stretch is what would go tidal (off the map, out of the freshwater rules).

DFO glossary, "tidal boundary": unless otherwise specified, a straight line between the two most seaward points of land on either side of the mouth of a river or stream (definitions).
