# T922 — Gear and Methods read as run-on sentences; "Roe" says it plainly

Status: OPEN.

Source: a tester's feedback relayed by the owner 2026-11-19 (screenshots `T922-shots/tester-2026-11-19-{1..4}.png`, Alice
Lake, Gear + Methods). Tester, verbatim: "The way things are formatted here makes it seem like a confusing run-on sentence.
Maybe start each new line with capital letters to separate different details or put periods at the end of each line or
piece of info. ... By just reading the section it was difficult to see you change the type of fishing from down riggers to
fishing with a flashlight." and "I know a lot of fishermen around here aren't native English speakers so it would read as
confusing". Owner replied: "Fair point I could break it up into a list or something."
On the bait line "Roe (fish eggs) — the fish or fish parts ban excepts it", tester: "This is worded a little weird"; owner:
"I guess most people think of roe as its own thing so I could probably just say roe." Tester: "Saying roe sounds better".

## Work
(a) Bait: the roe row reads "Roe" (or "Roe (fish eggs)") with no "the fish or fish parts ban excepts it" note.
(b) Methods / Gear sub-lines (e.g. Angling: "with a downrigger: attached to your line by a quick-release" / "with a light:
submerged, attached to the line, within 1 m of the hook"; Ice fishing: "lines: 1", "hook, lure or fly per line: 1"): each
condition is its own item, starting with a capital and ending with a full stop, visibly separated (a small bullet or
indent per item), so a reader sees where one condition ends and the next starts. Words composed in core (AGENTS 33).
Same on app and website. Pictures (Alice Lake Gear + Methods, 390 light/dark) to the owner before it lands.
## Acceptance
Whole-string tests on the composed lines; pictures approved by the owner.
Depends: none. Size: M. Owner: yes (look at pictures). Default: (a) "Roe (fish eggs)", no note; (b) one capitalised,
full-stopped line per condition.
