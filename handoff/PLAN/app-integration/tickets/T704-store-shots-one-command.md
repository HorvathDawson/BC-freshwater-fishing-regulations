## The command
`node app/tools/store-shots/run.mjs [--out DIR] [--at ISO] [--site URL]`. Defaults: staging site, clock noon B.C. today,
output `app/dist/store-shots/play-phone/` (8 PNGs, 1080x1920, `01-map` ... `08-09-offline`, plus `report.json`). It is a
standalone step in PRODUCTION.md "Every later release"; no build depends on it.

## What it uses
`app/tools/play-shots.mjs` (headless Chrome scenes, was already scripted) and `app/tools/store-shots.mjs` (framing and the
composed shots, already scripted); new: `app/tools/store-shots/run.mjs` (entry), `checks.mjs` (+ `checks.test.ts`).
Shot source = the STAGING WEB SITE, as the approved set was (no emulator, no APK; the web app is the same code).
- Pinned: page clock (`PIN_AT`, offset-based), light theme, 360x640 @3, notices seeded as seen, spot seed, waters by URL,
  Conditions camera at the 10 km scale.
- Not pinned, checked: live feeds (gauge reading, forecast, DFO salmon) and the site's release. Each scene's content is
  asserted on the page BEFORE the capture (`checks.mjs`): salmon (DFO) block above the game-fish calendar (shot 2), the CLEVER
  forecast on the chart, the Conditions maps at 10 km with coloured reaches, the river with more than one part. A failed
  check captures nothing, frames nothing, exits 1.
- Shot 2 composes salmon on top (store-shots.json order) since 0cba57a73.
