### 10. DFO feed stamp `at` only moves every ~24 h, and the app calls DFO stale at exactly 24 h: zero margin, daily false 'out of date'
Severity 2, wrong-or-missing-data, confirmed, reaches an angler; area dfo-salmon.
- Evidence: app/workers/feeds/src/dfo/live.ts:86 INDEX_HEARTBEAT_MS = 20 h; live.ts:318-325 + src/io.ts:128-145 skip the index.json write while content is unchanged and the last write is < 20 h old. Crons (wrangler.toml:77-78): every 6 h Jun-Oct, once a day (14:15 UTC) Nov-May. So writes land at T0 and T0+24 h in both seasons (6/12/18 h runs are skipped). app/packages/core/src/staleness.ts:60 dfo stale after DAY, read from the feed's `at` (menu/freshness.ts:76-80); the app re-asks only once per hour (data/src/feed/salmon.ts:36).
- Example: In-season: runs at 00:15, 06:15, 12:15, 18:15 all check DFO, but `at` stays 00:15 until the next day's 00:15 run finishes. A phone that fetched at 23:40 shows 'likely out of date' from 00:15 until its next hourly fetch; the card says 'Checked with DFO' ~24 h ago though it was checked 6 h ago.
- Second opinion: Mechanism is as described: with an unchanged page the 6/12/18 h runs skip, so `at` is rewritten only at +24 h in both seasons, exactly the stale line. ### 11. A missing salmon chain has three different answers in core (closed, null, throw)
Severity 2, fragility, partly; area app-logic-parity.
- Evidence: app/packages/core/src/salmon.ts:814-817 salmonStatusFromSpecies: `if (!c) return "closed"` (used by the page's year bar and closedOnly at :795-801, :846-856). :301-306 salmonChainStatus returns null (map paints nothing). :916-919 salmonTimelineKey throws SalmonError (added by T517 with the comment that a missing chain must be said, never ""). checkPayload (:176-198) proves indices inside chains but nothing proves chainOf(sid) < chains.length for a shard decoded against the head.
- Example: A section whose shard names chain k absent from the head: the map leaves it uncoloured, the stretch join throws, and the Salmon bar would draw a closed year.
- Second opinion: The three behaviours and the missing bounds check are real. - Proposed fix: salmonStatusFromSpecies throws SalmonError like salmonTimelineKey (the card already has a failed state); checkPayload/shard decode asserts every chainOf value is a real chain. One test per reader for a missing chain.
