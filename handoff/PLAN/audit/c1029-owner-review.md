# c1029: Burnaby Lake creeks, today vs after the rebuild (owner call)

Side build `data/generated/side/c1029`, held, nothing promoted. Full diff: `binding-diff-c1029.json`.

**The answer does not change.** Every creek below is **closed (No Fishing)** today and stays closed after the
rebuild. What changes is *which Region 2 row closes it*. All four rows are "No Fishing":

- **Still**: STILL CREEK (incl. tribs)
- **Brunette tribs**: BRUNETTE RIVER'S TRIBUTARIES
- **Burnaby Lake tribs**: BURNABY LAKE'S TRIBUTARIES
- **Brunette r1**: BRUNETTE RIVER "No Fishing upstream of Burnaby Lake …"

**Why it moves.** The city's Still Creek line ran *through* Burnaby Lake into the Brunette. Because of that, every
creek entering the lake counted as a Still Creek tributary, and also a Brunette tributary. With FWA restored, Still
Creek ends at Burnaby Lake and the tributary walk stops at the lake. Those creeks are now only Burnaby Lake's
tributaries, and the lake's own rows close them.

**Your call:** is that reading right, or should STILL CREEK / BRUNETTE TRIBUTARIES still claim the lake's inflows?
