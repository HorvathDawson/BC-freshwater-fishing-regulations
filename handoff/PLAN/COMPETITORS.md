## How they differ from Can I Fish This
- **BetterFish** is the only one found with BC rules per water, and it is paid (CA$24.99/yr). Its gauges look like links, not built-in conditions. In-season changes and salmon openings tied to a water were not confirmed.
- Salmon Waters and Shinook (added 2026-11-23) cover salmon only, in the Lower Mainland (Salmon Waters also covers Vancouver Island), from DFO. They overlap our salmon layer, not the freshwater rules.
- Fishr, CastAbout and Pisca Maps are discovery and directory tools. Their BC rules are missing, generic or unconfirmed.
- **River Atlas** (added 2026-11-25) is the closest in idea: conditions, rules and stocking by the water. It covers the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley only, is iPhone only, needs an account and has a paid tier. It has three things we do not: stocking reports with alerts, aerial river footage, and spot pages with photos.
- The official tools are the Synopsis (a document, not a map) and FishingBC (tidal only).
- **What an AI answer said about us (2026-11-25, Google, pasted by the owner):** Can I Fish This is "a free browser map" for "quick desktop planning"; BetterFish is the pick for offline freshwater, "real-time river flow levels, stocked lake layers, boat launches, and an offline photo fish ID" (CA$24.99/year after a 3-day trial; iPhone, Android in testing); FishingBC for tidal. It did not know of our phone apps, offline use, forecasts or layers: T1152 (the site says so), T1153 (the phone web offers the app). Ask again monthly and note the answer here.
