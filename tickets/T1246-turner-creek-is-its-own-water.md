0.6 km of it. It hangs under Noons Creek because the city file lists "Turner Creek" and "West Noons Creek" as alternative
names of Noons Creek. The city's own "Noons Creek" lies on top of the FWA one (the same creek mapped twice). The book's
rule: Noons Creek 2-8 Incl. Tribs, "No Fishing upstream of railway bridge".
