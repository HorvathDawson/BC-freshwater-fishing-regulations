## What that page gives (read 2026-11-23, GET)
City of Chilliwack, one gauge: the Fraser River at Mission (WSC 08MH024). A stage chart of level BANDS, each a number in
metres and a sentence on what the City does at it: below 5.5 m under bankfull (monitoring, emergency flood works finished);
5.5 m bankfull (the river fills its banks, regular dyke patrols); 6.0 m water spills onto unprotected floodplain; 7.0 m
extensive flooding outside the dykes, patrols round the clock; 8.0 m and over, water within about 0.6 m of some dyke crests.
It also lists upstream gauges (Prince George, Marguerite, Hope) with live readings but no bands.
- It is FLOOD guidance for the municipality (dykes, patrols), not fishing or wading guidance: nothing on wadeable, fishable or
  dangerous levels for an angler. It says its text was last updated May 2008 and sends readers to the Provincial River
  Forecast Centre for current advice.
- So the KIND of guidance is: named level bands per gauge, the number at each edge, and what each means. For us the bands
  would say what the level means for an angler (wading, boating, bank access), which this page does not.

## What to build (later)
A short "How to read this gauge" block on a gauge's page and in the app's Conditions tab, for a handful of gauges: the bands,
each edge, one sentence. OUR OWN WORDS, the source cited on the block (the agency or authority the bands come from: the
River Forecast Centre, the municipality or the gauge's operator), never copied; each band checked against its source. Never
an unsourced band: a gauge with no authority's bands gets no block (never a made-up threshold).

## Gauges to take first (to verify against the release's gauge list and each source)
Big, much-fished rivers whose authority publishes bands: Fraser at Hope (08MF005) and at Mission (08MH024, the source
above); Chilliwack/Vedder River at Vedder Crossing (08MH001); Cowichan River at Duncan (08HA011); Thompson River at Spences
Bridge (08LF051); Skeena River at Usk (08EF001). Pick by page views of their gauge pages once T1045 has been live a while.
